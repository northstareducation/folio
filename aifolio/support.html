Which Macs does Folio support?

The current Mac App Store edition targets macOS 14 or later on Apple Silicon, with at least 16 GB RAM. Allow storage for the app, its included model, a private writable model store and the statements you import. Processing speed depends on your Mac and the documents.

Do I need to install Ollama or download a model?

No separate installation is needed for the Mac App Store edition. Its local model and runtime are included. On first launch, the app verifies and prepares the model in its private data area. Open Settings & AI to see its readiness.

How do I import my first statement?
  1. Wait for the included local model to show AI ready.
  2. Choose Import statements and select a PDF, or drag it into the app.
  3. Review the extracted dates, merchants, amounts and categories.
  4. Approve the records when you are satisfied with them.

Use Save sample PDF in Settings & AI to try the fictional review statement. Its six rows produce SGD 101.48 net spending when the refund and card payment are treated correctly.

Why does extraction take time or need a correction?

Local AI uses your Mac's processing resources. Dense tables, scans and longer statements can take more work. Keep enough memory available and let the import complete. AI output can require corrections; review it before approval.

If an import fails, read the retained error details and use Retry extraction when appropriate. For support, share the error message and a fictional example rather than an unredacted statement.

Can it read scans and password-protected PDFs?

Selectable PDF text is read directly. Scanned pages can use the included Apple Vision OCR helper. If a PDF requires a password, provide it for that import. The entered PDF password is not saved by Folio or sent to the optional cloud model.

Can I add an expense that isn't in a PDF?

Yes. Choose Add transaction to enter a manual expense or refund. Manual records have their own source label and can be edited, removed from totals and restored. They do not need a PDF or a model request.

Can I change categories or export my records?

Review category changes before applying them, and use Undo when available. Transactions and Memory offer exports in CSV, Excel or JSON. Choose the export scope carefully: all-record exports can include draft, excluded and removed records.

Exports are useful for other applications, but they are not a complete backup of every workspace file, original PDF and setting.

Does it work offline?

Once the app and its included model are installed and prepared, local statement processing and questions work without an internet connection. Downloading the app or updates and using optional cloud AI or merchant web research require connectivity.

What happens if I enable cloud AI or merchant research?

Amazon Bedrock uses your own AWS account. Before an operation, the app describes the content to be shared and asks for confirmation. Global inference can process content worldwide.

Brave merchant research uses your own search API account and shows cleaned business-name queries for approval. These optional services may incur separate charges. Read the privacy policy for the data involved.

Is Folio a subscription?

No. The selected offer is a US$4.99 one-time app purchase with lifetime Folio access, future Folio updates and the included local AI. Regional pricing and availability are shown in the App Store. Optional AWS and search-provider charges are separate.

How do I reinstall the app or move to another Mac?

Use the Apple Account that purchased the app to download it again through the App Store. Your purchase and your local spending records are separate. Reinstalling or downloading on another Mac does not automatically transfer your workspace.

Use appropriate Mac backups for a restorable copy of your workspace. Export files can be used in other applications; Folio does not provide automatic cloud synchronization of your ledger.

How do refunds work?

Purchases and refund requests are handled by Apple in accordance with its policies and applicable consumer law. You can request help with an eligible purchase through Apple's Report a Problem service.

Do not send us your Apple Account password or payment-card details.

How do I back up or remove my data?

Folio stores records in its private local app-data folder. Back up the workspace before making permanent changes. Removing an entry from totals may retain it for Undo or Restore, and deleting the Mac app may leave its data folder behind.

To permanently remove the local workspace, quit the app and remove its app-data folder using macOS file management. Contact support if you need help identifying the correct folder. Copies in exports, backups or optional external services are managed separately. Read about your data choices.

Is my local workspace encrypted?

Folio does not add its own encryption to the local database or stored documents. macOS account permissions protect access, and FileVault can protect your disk at rest. Keep your Mac account and backups secure.

Why do some windows or menus say Folio?

Folio: Statement Insights is the App Store name. The installed app, its windows and menus use the shorter name Folio. These support instructions refer to the same app.

Check before relying on a result. Folio provides spending information and organization tools, not professional financial or tax advice. Review AI-generated transactions, categories and totals for accuracy.
