Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026.

Processing on your Mac

Folio uses local AI by default. In local mode, PDF contents, extracted transactions and conversations are processed on your Mac. The Mac App Store edition includes its local model and does not require a model download or a Folio account for local use.

Optional Amazon Bedrock processing

You may connect your own AWS account and select a supported cloud model. Before a cloud AI operation, Folio asks permission to send extracted statement text or your question, recent conversation, saved category rules, reviewed summaries and matching transaction details, including relevant manual entries and notes, to Amazon Bedrock. A category cleanup may review all matching merchant descriptions across several requests. This content can contain personal and financial information. PDF reading and OCR remain local; the PDF file and its entered password are not uploaded by this feature. Applying or undoing an existing category preview is local and does not make another model request.

The supported global inference profiles can process requests worldwide. Choosing a source region does not confine processing to that country. AWS and the selected model provider's terms, along with your AWS account settings such as invocation logging, govern cloud processing and retention. Folio does not claim that cloud data is handled solely on your device.

Folio's developer does not receive your AWS credentials or the content of these requests. IAM credentials are held in memory for the current app session and are cleared when the app quits. They are not included in the distributed app or written to project configuration files. AWS bills cloud usage separately from the Folio purchase.

Optional merchant web research

You may connect your own Brave Search API account. Folio shows cleaned public business-name queries for approval before sending them to Brave Search. Search requests exclude statement amounts, dates, account/card numbers, payment references and PDF contents. The queries can still reveal which businesses appear in your ledger. Brave receives ordinary connection information and handles queries under its terms and your account settings; provider usage charges may apply.

The selected local model or Bedrock model can use the search results to propose categories. When cloud AI is selected, the research confirmation covers sharing results and matching merchant details with that model. Folio's developer receives neither the queries nor your search key. Search keys are kept in memory for the session and cleared on quit. Saved category evidence can include source titles, URLs and retrieval times.

Local records

Original PDFs, extracted text, statement and manual transactions (including accounts, notes and removal status), merchant rules, category-change history, research-plan queries and source references, preferences and chat history are stored in Folio's private app-data folder. PDF passwords are used for the current import and are not saved. Removing an entry from totals may retain records needed for Undo or Restore. Data remains until you remove the stored workspace files. Folio's developer cannot access or delete these records remotely.

Exports, backups and removal

You can export all records or reviewed-only records as CSV, Excel or JSON from Transactions or Memory. Exports, operating-system backups and synchronization that you enable are under your control. Deleting the Mac app may leave its data folder behind. To permanently remove local records, first back up anything you want to keep, then quit Folio and remove its app-data folder using macOS file management. Folio does not encrypt its local database or PDFs; macOS FileVault can protect the disk at rest.

Analytics and purchases

Folio contains no analytics or advertising SDKs. App Store purchases and download transactions are handled by Apple under Apple's policies; Folio does not receive your Apple Account password or full payment-card details for the App Store purchase. Financial information within imported statements is handled as described above.

Other editions

Direct-download editions may offer optional model downloads. Their public model providers receive ordinary connection information needed to serve those files. Statement contents and financial memory are not included in those requests.

Support

If you contact help@northstareducation.io, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond to your request. Your email provider and ours process the message under their policies. Please avoid sending passwords, API keys, unredacted statements or sensitive financial details that are not needed for support.

For a privacy question or a request concerning a support message, contact the same address. We cannot remotely access or delete the workspace stored on your Mac.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They contain no analytics scripts, advertising, tracking cookies, forms or external fonts. GitHub may process ordinary connection information, such as IP addresses and browser details, to operate its hosting service. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Selecting an email link opens a draft in your email app. No message or statement is sent automatically. If you follow a link to Apple, AWS, Brave or another service, that service handles your visit under its own policies.

Changes and contact

This policy describes the current release. We will update the policy and its date if the app's data handling changes.

For support or privacy questions: help@northstareducation.io.
